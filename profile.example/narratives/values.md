# What I want from a team

**Honest measurement over confident narrative.** I would rather work somewhere
that publishes an uncomfortable number than somewhere that explains it away. The
teams I have learned most from were the ones willing to say "we don't know yet".

**Reviews as conversation, not gate.** The best code review I have had made my
change smaller. The worst were correct and useless — a list of things to fix
with no sense of whether the approach was right.

**Ownership that includes the pager.** I think the team that builds a thing
should run it, and I have found that belief survives contact with actually being
woken up. It changes what you build. But it only works when the load is
sustainable and the organisation treats the rotation as a real cost.

**Room to be wrong early.** I do my best work where a half-formed design note is
a normal thing to circulate rather than a sign you have not thought it through.

What I am looking for is a team where the interesting problems are load-bearing
rather than optional — where performance, cost and reliability are part of the
product rather than something to get to later.
