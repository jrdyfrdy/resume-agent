Quality Assurance Engineer I
Calloway Financial Systems
Location: Charlotte, NC (On site)

Position Summary
The Quality Assurance Engineer I is responsible for the design, execution and
maintenance of manual and automated test cases for our retail banking platform.

Essential Duties
- Author and execute detailed manual test plans against written requirements
- Maintain the regression suite in our test management system
- Log, triage and track defects through resolution
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Perform user acceptance testing coordination with business stakeholders

Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree
- 1-2 years of software quality assurance experience
- Demonstrated experience authoring manual test cases
- Familiarity with a test management system (qTest, Zephyr, or similar)
- Working knowledge of SQL for data validation
- Experience testing in a regulated financial environment

Preferred Qualifications
- ISTQB Foundation certification
- Exposure to Selenium or Cypress
- Understanding of ACH and card network settlement flows

Calloway Financial Systems is an equal opportunity employer.
