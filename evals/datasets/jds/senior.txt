Senior Engineer, Data Platform
Meridian Analytics — San Francisco or Remote

We build the analytics layer that our customers' data teams live in every day.
The Data Platform group owns ingestion, transformation, and the query engine
underneath the product. We're hiring a senior engineer to work on query
performance and cost.

Why this role exists
Our query volume tripled last year and our infrastructure bill grew faster than
our revenue. We need someone who is genuinely good at making expensive things
cheap — profiling, finding the real bottleneck, and shipping the fix — and who
can bring the rest of the team along while doing it.

What you'll work on
- Profiling and optimizing our distributed query engine
- Reducing cloud spend without degrading p99 latency
- Designing the storage and partitioning strategy for our columnar layer
- Setting the technical direction for the platform's next generation
- Reviewing designs across the group and raising the bar on what we ship
- Mentoring senior and mid-level engineers

What we're looking for
- 7+ years building data-intensive systems
- Deep experience with query optimization and performance profiling
- Strong SQL and a real understanding of how query planners behave
- Experience with columnar storage formats (Parquet, Arrow) or engines like
  DuckDB, ClickHouse, or Snowflake
- Comfort in Python; Rust or C++ for the hot paths is welcome but not required
- Experience owning cloud cost as an engineering problem, not a finance one
- A track record of technical leadership without formal authority

We care less about which specific technologies you've used than about whether
you can reason from first principles about where time and money are going in a
system.

How we work
Design docs before code for anything substantial. Code review is a conversation,
not a gate. We deploy continuously and we keep our own pagers — the team that
builds it runs it, and we've kept our on-call load light enough that this is
sustainable.

Compensation: $210,000 - $265,000 base, plus equity. We're remote-friendly and
have offices in San Francisco for those who want them.
