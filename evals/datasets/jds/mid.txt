Software Engineer II, Platform Infrastructure
Kestrel Health Systems
Location: Boston, MA (Hybrid — 3 days on site)

The successful candidate will join our Platform Infrastructure team, which owns
the services that every other engineering team at Kestrel depends on. Kestrel
processes clinical data for 400+ hospital systems, and our platform is the layer
that keeps that data moving reliably and within our regulatory obligations.

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate backend services that ingest and route clinical
  data at high volume
- Own services end to end, including participation in a shared on-call rotation
- Respond to production incidents, lead postmortems, and drive the remediation
  items that come out of them
- Improve the reliability and observability of existing services; our team
  publishes and defends internal SLOs
- Reduce operational toil through automation
- Partner with the Security and Compliance teams to ensure changes meet HIPAA
  requirements
- Mentor engineers earlier in their careers through code review

Required qualifications
- 3-5 years of professional backend engineering experience
- Proficiency in Python or Go
- Experience designing and operating RESTful services in production
- Strong working knowledge of PostgreSQL, including query optimization
- Experience with a message queue or streaming platform (Kafka preferred)
- Experience with containerized deployments (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Infrastructure as code experience, ideally Terraform
- Demonstrated experience carrying a production on-call pager

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with observability tooling (OpenTelemetry, Grafana, Prometheus)
- Familiarity with healthcare data standards (HL7, FHIR)
- Experience working in a regulated environment
- Experience with AWS

About the team
Platform Infrastructure is eight engineers. We take reliability seriously: we
run a blameless postmortem for every Sev1 and Sev2, we protect the on-call
rotation so that nobody carries the pager more than one week in six, and we hold
an error budget that the team — not management — decides how to spend.

Compensation: $145,000 - $175,000, plus equity and benefits. Kestrel is an equal
opportunity employer.
