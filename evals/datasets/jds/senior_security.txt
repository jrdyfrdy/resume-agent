Senior Application Security Engineer
Marchmont Trust

Marchmont Trust is a private bank. The Application Security team is responsible
for ensuring that everything we ship is defensible.

The role
- Lead threat modelling for new services
- Own our SAST and DAST tooling and triage its output
- Conduct code-level security reviews
- Run and remediate findings from external penetration tests
- Maintain our secure development lifecycle documentation
- Advise engineering teams on cryptographic design

Required
- 6+ years in application security
- Demonstrated experience with threat modelling methodologies (STRIDE or similar)
- Deep understanding of the OWASP Top 10 in practice, not as a checklist
- Experience with static and dynamic analysis tooling
- Familiarity with cryptographic primitives and their misuse
- Experience in a regulated financial environment

Preferred
- OSCP, CISSP or GIAC certification
- Experience with HSM key management
- Bug bounty triage experience
- Familiarity with PCI DSS
