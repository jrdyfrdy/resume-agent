Data Engineering Intern (Summer)
Weatherfield Institute for Climate Modelling

The Institute invites applications for a summer internship within the Data
Systems group. The successful candidate will assist in the maintenance and
extension of the ingestion pipelines that feed our regional climate models.

Responsibilities
- Assist in the development of Python-based ingestion routines
- Support the migration of legacy datasets into our columnar store
- Contribute to the documentation of data provenance
- Present findings at the end-of-programme colloquium

Required qualifications
- Currently enrolled in an undergraduate or master's programme in Computer
  Science, Atmospheric Science, or a related discipline
- Familiarity with Python
- Introductory coursework in databases

Preferred qualifications
- Exposure to Parquet or similar columnar formats
- Familiarity with scientific computing libraries
- Prior publication or conference presentation experience

The internship runs for twelve weeks and carries a stipend. Applicants should
submit a curriculum vitae and a brief statement of research interest.
