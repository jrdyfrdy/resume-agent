Embedded Software Engineer
Fenwick Instruments
Rochester, NY (On site)

Fenwick builds precision measurement instruments for semiconductor fabrication.
The Embedded Software group writes the firmware that runs them.

Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain firmware in C and C++ for ARM Cortex-M targets
- Write and debug device drivers for SPI, I2C and CAN peripherals
- Work alongside hardware engineers during board bring-up
- Optimize for deterministic timing under hard real-time constraints
- Support manufacturing test and field diagnostics

Required
- 3-6 years of embedded software development
- Strong C; working C++ for embedded targets
- Experience with an RTOS (FreeRTOS, Zephyr, or similar)
- Comfortable with an oscilloscope and a logic analyzer
- Understanding of memory-constrained development
- Experience with hardware bring-up

Preferred
- Semiconductor equipment domain experience
- Experience with IEC 61508 or similar functional safety standards
- Board design familiarity
- Python for test automation

This role is on site. The instruments are here and you cannot debug them
remotely.
