How your details are handled

This site builds resumes from a career file you write. This page covers everything it keeps, who can see it, where it goes, and how to remove it.

What is kept

  • From Google, when you sign in: your name, your email address, and Google's id for your account. The site asks Google for your basic profile and email address, and nothing else.
  • What you write: your career file (jobs, projects, achievements, skills and anything else you add) and every earlier version of each part, so a mistake can be undone.
  • What you make: for each resume, the company, the job title, how well you matched it, and the PDF, so you can download it again.
  • Chat conversations are held only in the server's memory, and disappear whenever it restarts.

Who can see it

  • Nobody else who signs in. Every page, file and download is tied to your account, and asking for someone else's finds nothing.
  • The person who runs this site. They see your name, email address and when you signed up, in order to let you in. And as with any website, they could read what is stored in its database.

Where it goes

  • To the AI provider, __PROVIDER__. When you make a resume or send a chat message, the job ad and the parts of your career file that task needs are sent to it, because that is what does the writing. Its own privacy policy covers what it receives.
  • To the services the site runs on: a web host and a database host, which store and serve it on the site's behalf.
    • __SIGNUP_NOTICE__
  • Nowhere else. No analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The pages load nothing from any other website, and matching your achievements to a job ad happens on the server itself.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, for up to two weeks. Your browser also remembers a couple of display settings, such as the Advanced switch. There is nothing else.

Removing it

  • Delete my account, in the menu under your email address, removes your account, your career file with every earlier version, and every resume you made. It happens at once and cannot be undone. If you are still waiting to be let in, the waiting page has the same button.
  • Two copies outlast that briefly. Answers cached while your resumes were being made stay on the server's temporary disk until it next restarts, which happens at every update and after a quiet spell. And the database host keeps a short rolling history for recovery, after which the data is gone for good.
